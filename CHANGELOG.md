# Changelog

All notable changes to this app. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/);
versions are the store versions (`expo.version`). Store "What's New" texts per language live in
`store/whats-new.json`.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- Checked against the deduplicated Pastelki library: every picture in the app is still published there, no picture changes.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-11-20

### Added
- Parents' area: links to rate Pasujki and to a short feedback page on pastelki.club (opens in the browser), and a one-time, gentle "Do you like Pasujki?" question for grown-ups. Everything stays behind the parental gate; the app itself still sends nothing.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-11-18

### Changed
- 72 pictures updated to newer versions from Pastelki: animals, fruit and food, toys, weather and everyday things.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-18

### Changed
- 45 animal pictures updated to newer versions from Pastelki; pictures now sync from the Pastelki library.
- Clearer illustrations of the zebra, birds, crow, giraffe and tiger.

### Fixed
- Added the expo-asset dependency that expo-audio needs.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-16

### Added
- First release: a calm matching game for children aged 2 to 4. A prompt (picture, colour, shape, shadow or pattern) goes to the matching corner by tap or drag.
- 68 levels in 8 worlds, including Shadows and the open-ended Make-believe world.
- 8 languages for the text parents see: Polish, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Dutch.
- Parents' area and privacy policy behind a parental gate. Fully offline, no data collected.
